Knoxx Privacy Policy

Updated November 10, 2026

Knoxx is an OpenHax workspace for AI-assisted chat, tools, and connected services. This notice explains how Knoxx handles information and how the connected Axxium identity service and the Knoxx audit archive use Google data.

Using Knoxx

When you sign in with GitHub, we use your GitHub account identifier and available profile details to identify your account and check your access. We also process account and organization details, session cookies, content and files you provide, tool requests and results, and operational and security records. We use this information to provide the workspace, manage access, run the features you choose, and investigate service or security issues. When a feature uses an external model or connected service, we send the content and metadata needed to fulfill the request to that provider.

Google sign-in through Axxium

If you choose Google sign-in through Axxium, Google supplies your account identifier, verified email address, and available profile information such as your display name. Axxium uses and stores the account identifier, email, and display name to link or create your account and authenticate you. It creates a session and uses a session cookie to keep you signed in. This sign-in does not grant Google Drive access.

Google Drive audit archive

A separate grant lets an authorized operator use Google Drive for Knoxx operational security records. The worker requests per-file access through drive.file. It creates encrypted batches in a designated private Drive folder and reads them back to verify delivery. These audit records may contain system and account identifiers, timestamps, and command or action details. Google receives the encrypted files and their Drive metadata. The worker uses the file identifiers and integrity checksums to record delivery, and keeps a protected refresh token to continue archiving between operator sign-ins.

Access, retention, and your choices

Authorized operators and the providers needed to deliver a feature process information for the purposes described here. We retain information as needed to operate the service, protect it, and support recovery. The archive worker acknowledges its local queue only after verifying delivery of the encrypted copy.

You can revoke future Google access in your Google Account connections. Revoking a connection and removing data already stored are separate actions. For questions about this policy or requests to access, correct, or delete your information, contact Knoxx support.
